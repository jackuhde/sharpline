# Sharpline NBA

Free NBA line-discrepancy app. Open the site on your phone and choose **Add to Home Screen** to install it.

- `index.html` is the app. `data.json` holds tonight's board and is refreshed automatically several times a day.
- Screenshot reading runs on the phone with Tesseract (files in `vendor/`), so nothing is uploaded.
- For research and entertainment only. Bet responsibly.
