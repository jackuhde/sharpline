Expert check · no influence today

Outside picks move Sharpline's numbers only with a record graded at the prices the expert actually posted, time-stamped before the game. The weight grows with sample size, ROI measured against its own noise, closing-line value, recency and quarter-to-quarter consistency, and is capped at 15% so the market price always leads. Win rate alone counts for nothing, because 65% on heavy favorites can still lose money. Under 200 graded picks, a losing record, or no closing-line value means zero weight.
